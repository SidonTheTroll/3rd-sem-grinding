## Machines in Welding
### Transformer
The voltage range employed in arc welding with an AC supply is normally 80 to 100 volts, but the main power supply in factories is at 440 volts. A device is therefore used to change this higher main-supply voltage to a lower voltage for welding. Such a device is called a transformer.

A transformer can also be used to raise the voltage. A transformer used to lower the voltage is termed a step-down transformer, and one used to raise the voltage is called a step-up transformer. The transformer used in AC welding plants is always the step-down type.

### Generator
A generator is a source of electrical energy. It produces electricity when it is run by a prime mover such as a turbine, engine, or motor. A generator may be AC or DC, giving AC or DC electricity respectively. The input to a generator is mechanical torque, and its output is electric current.

### Motor
A motor is the reverse of a generator: it is fed with electricity and converts electrical energy into mechanical energy. Its output is mechanical torque. A motor may be of the AC or DC type.

### DC Welding Motor Generator
This is a device used in electric arc welding that works with the help of a motor. It is a set consisting of a motor and a generator fitted on the same shaft. The motor of this set can easily be operated by an AC supply. In this set, the arc voltage is kept between 15 and 45 volts, while the open-circuit voltage, which makes striking the arc easy, is kept between 60 and 100 volts. A DC generator can supply a current of 20 to 1000 amps according to requirement.

### AC Motor Generator
In this set, a generator is driven by a suitable AC motor. The average voltage of the generator is 125 volts, and the current ranges from 25 to 100 amperes. The voltage can be adjusted to the desired value with the help of a rheostat.

### AC/DC Welding Rectifier
This is an electric arc welding device that can supply both AC and DC. It is a step-down transformer whose output is connected to a rectifier unit, which changes the AC supply to DC. The DC output is connected to the negative and positive terminals, and the supply for the welding process is taken from here through welding cables. It can be designed to provide either an AC or a DC welding supply with the help of an operating switch fitted on the rectifier machine.

## The Process of Gas Cutting
This process is employed for cutting steel up to a considerable thickness. The equipment essentially consists of a cutting blowpipe connected by hoses and regulators to one cylinder each of oxygen and acetylene gas. Oxy-acetylene gas cutting has two operations. First, a preheating flame is played on the base metal to be cut to raise it to red-hot condition. Second, a stream of high-pressure pure oxygen is applied to the hot metal, which oxidizes and cuts the metal.

### Flame
There are three kinds of oxy-acetylene flames:

1. Neutral flame
2. Carburizing flame
3. Oxidizing flame

#### 1. Neutral Flame
In this flame, equal amounts of oxygen and acetylene gas burn. It has a temperature of 3250 °C and is also called a soft flame. This flame is most commonly used for mild steel, low carbon steel, and high carbon steel.

#### 2. Carburizing Flame
In this flame, acetylene is in greater proportion to oxygen, and the inner cone is long with a feathery edge. This type of flame is used only on certain metals, because it increases the carbon content of the metal, making it hard and brittle. It is also called a reducing flame. Its temperature is 3100 °C. This flame is most commonly used on aluminium.

#### 3. Oxidizing Flame
In this flame, the gas coming out of the blowpipe has a large amount of oxygen, so it is used less in welding. The shape of this flame is like that of the neutral flame. The only difference is that the inner cone is shorter than that of the neutral flame, and it is pointed. The flame oxidizes the metal, and its color is blue. This flame makes a noise during combustion. Its temperature is up to 3300 °C.

## Brazing
Brazing is a process of joining two similar or dissimilar metals with the help of a filler metal. Various kinds of filler metal are used according to the metals being joined, to make the joint strong. The filler metal has a lower melting point than the adjoining metals.

## Soldering
Soldering is a process in which two similar or dissimilar metals are joined using a lower-temperature filler metal such as lead and tin. The melting point of the solder used should be lower than that of the job, so that the solder melts before the base metal does. Different kinds of solders are used for different work according to requirement.

### Types of Electrodes
There are three types of electrodes:

1. Bare electrode
2. Coated or covered electrode
3. Carbon electrode (the use of carbon electrodes is rare in welding)

### Types of Coated Electrodes
There are four types of coated electrodes:

1. Lightly coated electrode: the coating factor is 1.25 to 1.3
2. Medium coated electrode: the coating factor is 1.4 to 1.5
3. Heavy coated electrode: the coating factor is 1.6 to 2.2
4. Super heavy coated electrode: the coating factor is above 2.2

## Tungsten Inert Gas (TIG)
In this method, a tungsten electrode and filler rods are used. An inert gas such as argon or helium is delivered through a special type of torch. It is also known as TIG welding. Aluminum, carbon steel, iron, and other metals are welded by this method.

## Metal Inert Gas (MIG)
In this method, the arc is generated between the base metal and a metallic wire electrode. Carbon dioxide gas is used for shielding and is supplied through a special type of torch. Both automatic and semi-automatic methods are used. A DC supply is essential for these methods, and both ferrous and non-ferrous metals can be welded. It is also known as MIG welding.

- **Experiment no.:** 05
- **Object of the Experiment:** To find the deflection of a close coil helical spring, and to find the stiffness and modulus of rigidity of the material of the spring.

## Apparatus Required
A close coil helical spring with vernier attachment, outside caliper, and weights.

## Theory
The deflection of a close coil helical spring is given by:

$$
\delta = \frac{64 W R^3 n}{G d^4} \text{ cm}
$$

where,  
W = axial load in kg  
d = diameter of the spring rod in cm  
R = mean coil radius of the spring in cm = (D - d)/2, D being the outside diameter of the coil in cm  
n = number of turns of the coil  
G = modulus of rigidity of the material of the spring rod in $kg/cm^2$  
Stiffness (S) of the spring is defined as load per unit deflection, $S = W/\delta$ kg/cm

## Procedure
1. Measure the diameter of the spring rod (d) and the outside diameter of the coil (D) with the outside caliper.
2. Calculate the mean radius of the coil (R).
3. Note the number of turns of the coil of the spring.
4. Gently put the weight (W) on the hook of the spring, and note down the deflection from the vernier reading.
5. Calculate the stiffness and the modulus of rigidity of the spring material.

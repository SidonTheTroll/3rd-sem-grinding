- **Experiment no.:** 03
- **Title of the Experiment:** Compression Test
- **Object of the Experiment:** To determine the yield stress and ultimate crushing strength of the brick.

## Apparatus Required
Avery's compression testing machine, meter scale, bricks

## Theory
- Yield stress = load at yield point / area of compression (at first crack)
- Ultimate stress = ultimate load / area of compression (at fracture)

## Procedure
1. Measure the length and width of the brick (specimen) with a meter scale, and calculate the area of compression from them.
2. Place the brick flat at the center of the lower plate of the compression testing machine.
3. Turn the handle at the top of the spindle to lower the upper plate until it touches the top of the brick.
4. Tighten the supply valve of the oil contained at the bottom.
5. Operate the pumping handle steadily.
6. Note the load reading at which the needle jumps back at the first crack. This gives the yield point load.
7. Continue pumping steadily until the brick fractures and the needle swings back. Note the maximum value of the load (at which the brick fractures); this is the ultimate load.

## Observation
- Description of the brick specimen = Assam type brick
- Length of the brick, $l$ = 22 cm
- Width of the brick, $b$ = 10 cm
- Area of the brick, $A = l \times b$ = 220 $cm^2$

# Definition 
We have rewarded history, abundant cultures, literature, and social and community practices, defining what constitute Indian Knowledge. To better understand let's analyze each term separately. 

- **Indian:** by this term, we mean indigenous source of knowledge generated by Indian society. The term Indian points to undivided Indian subcontinent. 
- **Knowledge**: it is obtained by the insights gained by the personal experiences with real life situation. Facing problems and coming up with solutions constitute the knowledge. 
- **System**: system means structured methodology and the classifying scheme to access the available knowledge, making it applicable and accessible. 
# Do we need IKS? 
1. IKS is about protecting revised wisdom, ensuring economic security and national pride in our rich heritage. 
2. Challenging misconceptions such that all knowledge that benefits humanity originated from the west. 

- Ancient knowledge is kept in two ways:
	1. **Formal**: available in written form 
	2. **Informal**: passed from person to person
# Importance of IKS
1. **Economic value**: transforming knowledge into economic values for the benefit of the country and citizens. 
2. **Culture**: to preserve cultural activities of the society.
3. **Identity**: it defines the context for several aspects of day to day living of every individual.

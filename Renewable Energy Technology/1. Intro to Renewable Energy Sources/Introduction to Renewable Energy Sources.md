Renewable Resources feeds and grow back fast in nature. They never run out. Non-renewable resources take millions of years to form. THey stay in limited supply and disappear once used.
## Classification of Energy Sources 
1. Renewable (solar, wind, geothermal, tidal) and Non-renewable (coal, petroleum, natural gas)
2. Primary and secondary 
3. Conventional and non-conventional 
## Forms of Energy 
1. Kinetic energy and potential energy 
2. Thermal and chemical energy 
3. Radiant and nuclear energy 
## Energy Chain or Energy Routes 
In ecology, an energy chain commonly called the food chain is a straight single path of energy transfer where one organism eats another. 
Energy routes is also called food web or energy flow pathway describes the complex, interconnected network of multiple feeding relationships and paths in an ecosystem. 
In engineering, an energy chain is a physical, hollow track that guides and protects moving hoses and electrical cables on industrial machines. 
## Electric Energy from Conventional Sources 
### Thermal Plant (Rankine Cycle)
A thermal power plant generates electrical energy from a conventional, non-renewable source by burning fossil fuels like coal, natural gas or oil to heat the water. This creates high pressure steam that spins a turbine, driving a generator to produce electricity for the powergrid. Major operators like Tata Power rely on these plants for dependable base load power. 

- How the process works: 
	1.  Fuel combustion 
	2. Steam production 
	3. Mechanical rotation 
	4. Electricity generation 
	5. Condensation and recycling 
## Integrated Gasification Combined Cycle 
An IGCC is an advanced thermal power station that converts solid fuels like coal into synthetic gas. It cleans the gas before burning it in a gas turbine and uses waste heat to drive a steam turbine, acheving higher efficiency and lower emissions than standard coal plants.

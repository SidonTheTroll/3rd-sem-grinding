## Simple Stresses and Strains 
Force or moment can bring a change in rest/uniform velocity, acceleration, deformation and/or internal force. 
![[IMG_20260809_134545~2.jpg|433]]![[IMG_20260809_134549~2.jpg|476]]

For change in deformation and internal force, we must consider the body to not be rigid. 

> [!note]
> Axial load is along longitudinal axis and shear/transverse load is along transverse axis. 
> > There can be infinite transverse axis. 
>
>Twisting/torsional moment is along longitudinal axis and bending moment is along transverse moment.

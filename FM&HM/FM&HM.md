Fluid is a substance which is capable of flowing or continuously deforms under shear (tangential force).

- Fluids are of 3 types 
	1. Liquid (incompressible)
	2. Gas (compressible)
	3. Vapor (substance in gas phase at temperature lower than its critical temperature)

[[Hydraulic Turbines & Pumps]] can be classified into two types: 
1. Power generating mahcines (turbines)
2. Power absorbing machines (pumps)
